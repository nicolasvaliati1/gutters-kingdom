## Technical claims already used on-site

- Pitch: about a quarter inch per ten feet toward the downspout.
- Hidden hangers every 24 inches or closer.
- One downspout per 30 to 40 feet of gutter is a common rule of thumb.
- A 3x4 downspout has twice the opening area of a 2x3 (12 vs 6 square inches).
- 5-inch is the standard gutter size; 6-inch is for large roof planes, steep pitches, and heavy rain.
- Aluminum gutters last 20+ years; copper 50+ years.
- Copper must not touch aluminum or galvanized steel (galvanic corrosion).
- Repair vs replace: replace when more than about a third of the run needs work.
