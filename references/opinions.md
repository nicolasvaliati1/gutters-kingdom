## Where the water lands matters more than which gutter you buy

A gutter that empties beside the foundation does more damage than no gutter at all. Moving or extending a downspout is often cheaper than replacing the system.

**Backed by:** downspout outlet guidance and the $150 – $1,200 downspout range (`stats.md`).
**Use for:** downspout posts, wet-foundation posts, "do I need new gutters" posts.

## The cheapest quote is usually thin gauge on widely spaced hangers

A quote that's far below the others usually assumes .027-inch aluminum and wide hanger spacing. The gutter looks the same on install day and sags within a few years.

**Backed by:** hanger spacing of 24 inches or closer; gauge difference .027 vs .032 inch (`stats.md`).
**Use for:** "why do gutter quotes differ" posts, hiring posts, estimate-checklist posts.

## Gutter guards reduce cleaning, they don't end it

Every guard style lets something through, and some styles overshoot in a heavy downpour. Plan on an inspection once or twice a year regardless.

**Backed by:** guard price range $1,000 – $4,500 versus cleaning at $120 – $450 (`stats.md`).
**Use for:** gutter guard posts, cost-comparison posts.
